I really find it meaningful to gather people together, to foster community 
and share experiences and knowledge, to learn from each other. 
Because of this I’ve been producing numerous of events – 
both as part of my work as a designer, as well as on my free time. 
This has been anything from managing all aspects from concept development, 
planning, promoting and speaker and artist booking to guest experience and 
social media engagement.
